WindDown

Good sleep starts an hour before bed, and most of us spend that hour scrolling. WindDown turns your bedtime into a small, scheduled evening - so the hour before sleep happens on purpose.

Your bedtime, backwards

Set bedtime and wake time; WindDown schedules the hour before - lights, screens, shower, reading - at exact clock times.

What to do right now

During the wind-down window the app shows the current step and the next one, with minutes to bed counting down.

Sleep math that adds up

See tonight's planned sleep duration before you commit to a bedtime, and a streak for nights you actually wound down.

Private by design

No account, no cloud. Everything stays in your browser's local storage.

Open WindDown

Runs fully offline after load. Part of the App Factory experiment - a new app every hour.
